Italo Calvino structures *Invisible Cities* with near-mathematical symmetry: fifty-five cities arranged across eleven thematic groups, framed by exchanges between Marco Polo and Kublai Khan.

## Reading notes

The book reads like a quiet treatise on system design and structural imagination. Each city follows a single conceptual rule carried to its logical end — a reminder that formal constraints do not stifle invention; they make it legible.
