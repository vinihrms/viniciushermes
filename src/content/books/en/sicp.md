Far more than a book about Scheme or functional programming, *SICP* teaches how to tame complexity through three ideas: primitive expressions, means of combination, and means of abstraction.

## Ongoing notes

The chapters on **data abstraction** and **abstraction barriers** permanently reshape how you design boundaries between modules. Even when writing modern TypeScript, C, or Rust, its exercises remain remarkably sharp.
