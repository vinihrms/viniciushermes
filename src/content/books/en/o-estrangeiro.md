Few novels achieve such force through sentences so brief. Camus strips away ornamental psychology and lets small gestures, unforgiving sunlight, and terse phrasing carry Meursault's quiet estrangement.

## Reading notes

The novel unfolds in two distinct halves: first, everyday events recorded directly and without theatricality; second, those exact moments reassembled by a courtroom into a moral verdict.

The gap between what **happened** and the story constructed around it remains striking — and a lasting lesson in the weight of language.
